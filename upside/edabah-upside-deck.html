04 · Problem Statement

Why fuel-only users aren't adopting grocery

Three root-cause areas. All are valid — but the Data & Personalization gap is the foundational bet because without understanding customers, we can't solve the other two effectively. (Click to expand each.)

1 Awareness & Education Gap
▶

Don't know grocery cash back exists on Upside; don't understand the savings even if aware.

Why it matters: Cheap to solve with banners and campaigns — but awareness alone doesn't change behavior without personal relevance. Low-hanging fruit with diminishing returns.

2 Switching Resistance
▶

Habit/loyalty to current grocer, unfamiliar merchant names reduce trust, offer not compelling enough, bigger/riskier spend to redirect than gas, merchant coverage gap, added cognitive overhead.

Why it matters: Real barrier, but hard to isolate or address without knowing which specific resistance each customer faces — which requires data.

3 Data & Personalization Gap Foundational Bet
▶

Limited insight into what a customer actually likes — frequency, product preference, delivery vs. in-store, who they buy for, seasonality. Blocks fixing Switching Resistance in a targeted way.

Why this is the foundational bet: We're not understanding customers well enough to serve them. Generic awareness is cheap to solve, but without personalization data, awareness messages aren't worth acting on. Data & personalization is a leading cause for both awareness ineffectiveness and switching resistance.

"The real gap is that we don't know enough about a given customer to make the awareness message worth acting on — which merchant to name, what incentive to offer, whether they'd even shop groceries there at all."

08 · Risks

Key risks and de-escalation criteria

Loyalty vs. Incrementality Paradox

Upside's model rewards consumer switching (chase the best deal this week), but merchants may expect durable, loyal customers. "Cash back" implicitly signals loyalty, but the marketplace drives switching behavior.

Resolution: Incrementality is measured per-merchant, independently — each merchant only pays for provably incremental visits. This is a network-growth strategy, not a loyalty promise.
De-escalation: If merchant churn or complaint rate rises >5% WoW, pause new grocery merchant onboarding and diagnose expectation gap. Merchant retention/satisfaction guardrail in Success Metrics.

Fuel Cannibalization

Diverting attention and incentive budget to grocery could reduce fuel engagement — the core revenue driver today.

Resolution: Stack & Save bundling is designed to reinforce fuel (higher fuel cash-back when paired with grocery), not compete with it.
De-escalation: If fuel MAU declines >2% in test cohort, pause grocery incentive campaigns for that segment and review. Explicit guardrail in Success Metrics.
